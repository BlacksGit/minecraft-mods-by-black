# Minecraft mods by Black

Free Fabric mods for Minecraft 26.2. Support development: https://patreon.com/123black

| Mod | What it does | Download |
|---|---|---|
| Portal Gun and Chambers | Portal 2 style portal gun with live portal views, plus test chamber dungeons built from 90 hand-made puzzles | [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/portal-gun-and-chambers), [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/portal-gun-26) |
| Rain and Water | Realistic rain, reflective water, wet surfaces, puddles, wet-looking players and mobs | [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/rain-and-water) |
| Sky and Weather | Scattering sky, volumetric clouds, aurora, wind, storms, drifting snow, footprints, seasons | [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/sky-and-weather) |
| Fallen Star: Starfall Events | Stars fall on rare nights; race to the landing site, then fight the Star Sovereign boss | [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/fallen-star-starfall-events) |
| Tale of Kingdoms: The Shattered Crown | RPG campaign: guild, 105-quest main story, 30 spells, fifteen towns | [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/tale-of-kingdoms) |
| Special Mobs Revived | Unofficial Fabric 26.2 port of FatherToast's Special Mobs: 148 variants of vanilla monsters | [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/special-mobs-revived) |
| LagLens | See which chunks and mobs are killing your TPS, then click to teleport there | [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/laglens) |
| CapLimit | Caps hoppers and mobs per chunk to stop lag, with an in-game GUI and no YAML editing | [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/caplimit) |

Also: free guides for 3D printing, budgeting and home servers at https://blacksgit.github.io
